FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does my data go?

Your products and costs are stored securely by Margino so it can price them for you, and you can keep a live copy in a Google Sheet that you own. Margino only ever touches the Sheet it created, and you can delete your account and data anytime in Settings.

How are the platform prices calculated?

Margino starts from your real unit cost and works up to a price that holds your target margin after typical delivery-platform commissions, so your in-store, Uber Eats, and DoorDash prices each stay profitable.

Do I need to type anything?

No. Snap a photo of the receipt or invoice and Margino reads the line items for you. You get a quick review screen to confirm before it syncs.

What does it cost to start?

It's free to start, no credit card. Upgrade in-app for more receipts, dish costing and Monday price-hike alerts.

Does it work with my POS?

Square: yes, starting with the next update. Connect it in Settings and your dishes follow your Square menu prices. Other systems: Margino works without a POS, since everything starts from your receipts.

Which devices are supported?

Margino is an iPhone app, available now on the App Store in 175 countries.
